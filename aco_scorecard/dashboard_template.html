Sources. Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, Medicare Shared Savings Program public use files on data.cms.gov: Performance Year Financial and Quality Results (PY 2013 to 2024, 13 files) and Number of ACO Assigned Beneficiaries by County (PY 2016 to 2024, 10 files). Aggregate ACO-level data only; CMS suppresses cells with 1 to 10 beneficiaries. This page is an independent analysis and is not affiliated with or endorsed by CMS.

Read with care. Quality scores are comparable only within a scoring era (2013 to 2020 ACO measure set; 2021 to 2024 APM Performance Pathway, with a health equity adjustment in 2023). PY 2020 and 2021 spending excludes COVID-19 episode months. PY 2013 covers 18 or 21 months for ACOs that started in 2012. Net savings to Medicare here is benchmark minus spending, less shared savings payments, plus losses owed; CMS's own published figures may use other adjustments.
